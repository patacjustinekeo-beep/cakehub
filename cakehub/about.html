Our Story

Made with passion, baked with love.

CakeHub Tagbilaran was founded in 2026 in Tagbilaran City, Bohol. We create custom cakes for birthdays, weddings, christenings and every celebration worth remembering.

Each cake is designed with you: the theme, the colors, the size and the small details. Then it is baked fresh for your date and finished by hand.

Our Philosophy

Mission

Vision

To be the cake shop people in Bohol think of first for their most important moments.

Craftsmanship

Every tier, border and topper is made by hand with care for the details.

Quality ingredients

We choose premium ingredients for a taste you will love.
